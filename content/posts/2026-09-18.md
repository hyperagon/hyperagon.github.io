Here's an interesting tidbit of information;
What did I last install using **apt-get**? [AskUbuntu had an entry aabout it.](https://askubuntu.com/questions/17012/is-it-possible-to-get-a-list-of-most-recently-installed-packages)

```
awk '$3~/^install$/ {print $4;}' /var/log/dpkg.log
```

Gave me just what I needed.

```
ncdu:amd64
```

I knew it was **du** (disk usage) something, [NCDU](https://dev.yorhel.nl/ncdu) is a handy tool to see where the disk space is used,
