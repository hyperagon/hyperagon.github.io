After so many videos talking English I got comments mentioning [Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veracruz)
and the [Amenaza](https://content.luanti.org/packages/ronrob-lu/amenaza/) game.

I picked dances that match their names and [Fandango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fandango) is a Spanish dance,
so either that or [Flamenco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamenco) (which is also Spanish).

So is *Fam* Spanish? Sure, her options both point in that direction.
