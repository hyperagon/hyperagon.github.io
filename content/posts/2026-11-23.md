Trying to add Add-ons to Blender didn't work as expected.

In this case I'm talking about [VectArt](https://github.com/Dream-Pixels-Forge/add_on_vectart_import),
as usual it is not compatible with version [5.2](https://www.blender.org/releases/5-2/).

But at least [Screencast Keys](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/screencast-keys/) works.

While [Rhubarb Lip-sync](https://github.com/DanielSWolf/rhubarb-lip-sync) is 2D you can just [(Deform >) Shrink-wrap](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/shrinkwrap.html) the textured plane (2D) onto a 3D object.
