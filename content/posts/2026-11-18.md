I'm at a point where I can mostly use [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/) for 2D animation but it's a lot of trouble so
wouldn't it be better to make things in 3D? The rotation will require me to imagine what it'd look like anyway.

While [Rhubarb Lip-sync](https://github.com/DanielSWolf/rhubarb-lip-sync) is 2D you can just [(Deform >) Shrink-wrap](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/shrinkwrap.html) the textured plane (2D) onto a 3D object.

One thing that I love/hate is how smooth grease pencil strokes are, as I'd rather use minimal points to edit later.

I was tried splitting arms into two parts but that's unnecessary, if anything it's more trouble that it's worth. And forget about exporting then importing SVG, I tried with the mouth and couldn't replace the grease pencil layer.
