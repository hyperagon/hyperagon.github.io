I'm at a crossroads, *CopyLEFT* (GPL/AGPL) ensures that code remains open while *CopyRIGHT*, in this case BSD/MIT,
is basically free-for-all.

These two ideologies don't mix [as well as you'd expect](https://fossforce.com/2026/07/freebsd-16-cleans-house-no-gpl-left-in-the-base-system/).

I vibe coded [WebVG](https://github.com/hyperagon/WebVG) and gave it the [AGPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Affero_General_Public_License) license but considering how a large part of the code was generated by *LLM*s doesn't that mean [that it is public domain code](https://law.stackexchange.com/questions/107474/what-happens-when-code-written-with-genai-is-open-sourced)?

In comparison the licenses have a very different size so maybe I'll go for the shorter one.
I changed the license for [WebVG](https://github.com/hyperagon/WebVG), now it's more in line with what I got planned for the future.
